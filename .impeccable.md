## Design Context

### Users
Visitors explore a student group's portfolio through a conversational interface. They need to quickly understand who the team members are, their strengths, and the projects they have built, while being able to ask follow-up questions naturally.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, discerning, and calm. The product should feel like a thoughtful creative curator: confident and polished, but never technical, cluttered, or loud.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the supplied **Editorial Intelligence** system as the visual source of truth: an off-white gallery canvas, graphite ink, selective terracotta and azure accents, Playfair Display for editorial moments, and Plus Jakarta Sans for interface text. Preserve a light, pill-centric interface with subtle glass layers and feather-light elevation. The layout must reflow from a desktop navigation rail to a compact mobile experience.

### Design Principles
1. Make the conversation and portfolio content the visual focus; navigation and controls stay quiet.
2. Use generous whitespace, precise typography, and low-contrast surfaces in place of heavy cards and borders.
3. Reserve colour for meaningful modes, status, and actions.
4. Keep controls tactile and legible, with clear focus, hover, loading, and reduced-motion states.
5. Adapt the information architecture for small screens instead of merely shrinking the desktop layout.
